Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about industrial visit bookings and safety norms.

Eligibility is configured per visit based on relevance to academic branches and year of study. When browsing visits, the portal automatically indicates whether your profile is eligible.

The fee generally covers college-organized air-conditioned bus transit, security clearance processing, PPE (safety gear), industrial lunch / refreshments, and an official participation certificate.

Cancellations can be made directly through the Student Dashboard up until 48 hours before the scheduled visit date. Refunds are processed according to college guidelines.

Manufacturing facilities strictly mandate fully enclosed leather shoes or safety boots, formal or college uniform attire, and physical college ID cards. Photography inside secure plant floors is strictly restricted.
